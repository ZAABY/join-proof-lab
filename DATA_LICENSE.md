The optional real-data study uses Olist's Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset:

- Original data card: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce/

The original data is not included here. Downloaded inputs must remain excluded from git. Olist-derived results, charts and study artifacts must carry their own CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice, attribution and description of changes. The MIT code license does not remove the noncommercial or share-alike conditions for those materials. No Olist endorsement is implied.

The study constructs joins and explicit pre-aggregation alternatives from the published records. Its intentionally incorrect queries are our experimental scenarios, not claims about Olist's production analytics. No raw review text should be republished. Data is a historical anonymized dataset, not current sales information.

A commercial user can use the MIT engine with appropriately licensed data. This repository grants no permission for commercial reuse of the Olist dataset or its derived artifacts.
